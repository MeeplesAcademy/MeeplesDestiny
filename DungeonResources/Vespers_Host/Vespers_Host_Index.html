Activation 1st Encounter

Killing the Brig in the Main Room unlocks the Heart, Lungs, and Brain side rooms. In any order, enter a room, use the Scanner augment to reveal which panels are not safe to shoot and then use the Operator augment to shoot & activate the safe panels to spawn a bomb. Return to the Main Room, kill the next Brig to unlock a depository, and deposit the bomb. Repeat for all three rooms — the third return is guarded by a Boss Brig.

Each room has 6 panels. 4 are correct, 2 are wrong. Mark the two wrong panels below — the remaining four are the ones to shoot.

Dismemberment 2nd Encounter · Raneiks Unified

Fight adds until a Machine Priest spawns and dies, teleporting you upstairs where Raneiks divides into 10 servitors. Two of them light up circles that encode a number 1–9 — note both, then repeat the trip for two more. Once all four numbers are known, shoot the matching panels downstairs to drop the shield and trigger the damage phase.
